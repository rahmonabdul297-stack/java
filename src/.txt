Build a console application that allows a user to track their daily expenses.

Your program should:

Ask for the user's name.
Ask how much money they have.
Allow them to enter expenses.
Store the expenses in an array.
Calculate:
Total expenses
Remaining balance
Average expense
Largest expense
Smallest expense
Display a simple financial summary.

Concepts to practice:
Scanner, variables, data types, operators, if/else, loops, arrays, methods.
